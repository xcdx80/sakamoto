# Sakamoto

Sakamoto is a personal knowledge library of saved articles, documents and X posts. Readers can read their saved material and ask questions answered with citations to that material.

## Language

**Library item**:
A saved article, document or post in a reader's library.
_Avoid_: Book, when referring to all saved content

**Article**:
A published text saved from the web as a library item.

**Document**:
A text saved from a file or pasted content as a library item.

**Post**:
An individual X post, with its author, text and original location.
_Avoid_: Article, when referring to an X post

**Bookmark**:
A reader's saved reference to a post on X. The bookmark and the post it refers to are distinct.

**Author thread**:
A connected sequence of posts by the same author, linked through replies.
_Avoid_: Conversation, which can include replies by other people

**Context post**:
A parent or quoted post needed to understand a saved post. Its author may differ from the author of the saved post.

**Linked article**:
A web article directly linked from a saved post.

**Linked document**:
A document, such as a PDF, directly linked from a saved post.

**Library answer**:
An answer to a reader's question grounded in material saved in their library.

**Citation**:
A reference from an answer to the saved material supporting a claim, with enough information to locate the supporting passage or post.

**Saved passage**:
A portion of a saved source, with its original location and source revision, that can support a library answer or be opened from search.

**Evidence search**:
Finding supporting saved passages by meaning or exact wording within the reader's eligible library.
