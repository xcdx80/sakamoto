# Independent review establishes draft provenance

Date: 2026-11-07
Status: Accepted

Writer citation annotations are hints. Two models can describe the same claim differently, so requiring the reviewer to repeat the writer's annotation makes otherwise reviewable drafts stop before factual validation. A live software-factory revision cited `An AI factory…` while its actual post began `AI factory…`.

Preserve the published wording. Discard new writer hints that cannot locate an actual post or alternate-hook span; never invent a fuzzy match. Historical saved hints remain auditable but grant no evidence for wording they do not address.

For generated and author-written drafts alike, independent review may establish provenance from exact actual draft spans, known evidence IDs, exact inspected source-excerpt quotes, and substantive semantic explanations. Every independently identified factual assertion requires complete fact-kind support at its actual post or hook address; several exact proof spans may cover one longer factual assessment without changing words or inferring support for gaps.

When a retained writer hint contains an independently assessed factual clause, or is contained within that clause, the independent factual assessment owns its proof obligation at each actual address. Do not demand invented evidence for nonfactual labels or closing questions surrounding that clause. Attribution still belongs in the independent factual audit: an attribution label is not automatically nonfactual. Every identified factual clause, including any second clause or factual premise within a question, remains binding. Hints without that factual relationship still need complete interpretive proof. Historical reviews without factual assessments retain literal-hint proof checks. Missing proof, unsupported author experience, author ideas used as facts, and confident independent gate failures continue to block approval.

Every alternate hook receives its own complete factual assessment, identifying its exact factual clauses independently of its question or opinion. Those clauses require fact-kind source proof. A partial numeric or label annotation cannot exempt the surrounding assertion or borrow the published post's classification. Opinions and questions may be nonfactual only when they contain no embedded externally checkable assertion.

This changes provenance ownership, not approval: saved wording stays immutable, review pins use a new prompt version, and only the author can approve the exact reviewed revision. Keep strict failures for malformed structures, unknown evidence IDs, and invented source quotes.
